# 🛒 Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

Customer behavior analysis helps businesses understand purchasing patterns, customer preferences, and factors influencing sales performance. This project focuses on transforming raw customer shopping data into actionable business insights using data analytics and visualization techniques.

## Tools Used

**SQL** – Data analysis and querying
**Power BI** – Dashboard creation and visualization
**Python (Pandas, NumPy)** – Data cleaning and exploratory data analysis (EDA)
**Excel/CSV** – Data source

## SQL Analysis Performed

The following business questions were analyzed using SQL:

1. Which category generates the highest revenue?

2. Are discounts actually increasing purchase value?

3. What is the total revenue generated by male and female customers?

4. Which customers used a discount but still spent more than the average purchase amount?

5. Which are the top 5 products with the highest average review ratings?

6. Which are the bottom 5 products with the lowest average review ratings?

7. What is the average purchase value and total revenue by shipping type?

8. Do subscribed customers spend more? Compare average spend and total revenue between subscribers and non-subscribers.

9. Which are the top 5 products with the highest discount usage percentage?

10. How are customers segmented into New, Returning, and Loyal customers based on previous purchases?

11. What are the top 3 most purchased products within each category?

12. Are repeat buyers (more than 5 previous purchases) more likely to subscribe?

13. What is the revenue contribution of each age group?

## Key Insights

* Certain product categories contribute significantly more revenue than others.
* Discounts influence customer purchasing behavior and spending patterns.
* Subscribers tend to exhibit different purchasing habits compared to non-subscribers.
* Highly rated products often generate stronger customer engagement.
* Shipping preferences impact purchase decisions and customer satisfaction.
* Customer demographics provide valuable insights for targeted marketing.
* Repeat purchasing behavior highlights customer loyalty trends.
